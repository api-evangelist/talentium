# Talentium

> Talentium is an end-to-end, AI-powered hiring platform that automates candidate sourcing, screening, and outreach — helping founders, in-house talent teams, and recruitment agencies fill roles faster.

Talentium unifies the recruitment workflow in one place: AI sources candidates (including technical talent from channels like GitHub), screens and ranks them, and runs personalized outreach at scale. It is GDPR-compliant with enterprise-grade security and is available on the Google Cloud Marketplace. The site is published in English, Dutch (/nl/), and German (/de/).

## Features

- [AI Search](https://talentium.io/features/ai-search/): Describe the role in plain English; Talentium ranks candidates from public sources and your network with a reason for every match.
- [Outreach](https://talentium.io/features/outreach/): AI-drafted first messages and follow-up sequences, sent from your own inbox, with reply tracking.
- [Candidate Management](https://talentium.io/features/candidate-management/): A visual pipeline per role with auto-status, notes, and share-by-link for hiring managers.
- [Career Page & Job Boards](https://talentium.io/features/career-page/): A branded careers page on your domain in minutes, with one-click distribution to top job boards.
- [Network & Integrations](https://talentium.io/features/network-integrations/): Your living talent pool — saved candidates, applicants, CSV imports, and ATS history in one searchable view.
